<section class="vp-section">
  <h2 class="serif vp-h2">What it is</h2>
  <div class="vp-prose">
    <p>Most people in Namibia reach the internet through WhatsApp, not a browser. So Ongiini lives where
      people already are: a free assistant you message like any other contact, in the language you actually
      think in. No app to download, no account to create, no fee at the door.</p>
    <p>It is the first project of the Common Intelligence Foundation — a non-profit, currently being
      formally established, built on a simple conviction: intelligence is a common good, and useful AI
      should not be something that happens <em>to</em> a community from somewhere else.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<blockquote class="art-quote vp-quote">
  <Icon name="quote" size={28} cls="art-quote-mark" />
  <p class="serif">Where intelligence lives, and in whose language it speaks, decides who stays capable.</p>
</blockquote>

<section class="vp-section">
  <h2 class="serif vp-h2">Why it matters</h2>
  <div class="vp-prose">
    <p>An assistant that does not speak Oshiwambo well is not a smaller version of the same tool — it is a
      different tool, one that quietly tells you your language is a second-class way to think. Ongiini
      answers in English and Afrikaans today; Oshiwambo is being built in as core infrastructure, not
      bolted on later. Language is not a feature here. It is the foundation.</p>
    <p>Where the hardware sits is the same decision in physical form. Sovereignty is not a slogan you can
      hold while the servers, the data, and the value all flow somewhere else. The pilot runs on one small
      machine we operate ourselves — no US cloud provider anywhere in the pipeline — and the goal is to
      move it to Namibia as soon as the service is sustainable.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="vp-section">
  <h2 class="serif vp-h2">How it holds to the principle</h2>
  <div class="vp-prose">
    <ul class="vp-principles">
      <li><span class="vp-pk">Non-extractive</span> Value, data, and capability stay close to the
        community. The project is structured so that it cannot quietly become an extraction pipeline.</li>
      <li><span class="vp-pk">Language-preserving</span> Every interaction strengthens, rather than
        erodes, a living language — the opposite of the homogenising pull most technology exerts.</li>
      <li><span class="vp-pk">Self-operated by design</span> No hyperscaler in the loop, and a clear path
        to hardware located where it is used — sovereignty as a fact of the architecture, not a promise
        in a charter.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>
